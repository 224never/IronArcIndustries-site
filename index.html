IronArc Industries is developing command, intelligence and interoperability software for autonomous systems. Built around FORGE, TEMPER and KASSCORE, our direction is platform-agnostic coordination across third-party air, ground and surface vehicles and external command systems.

ONE MISSION.
DISCONNECTED SYSTEMS.

Operators work across vehicles, sensors and command tools that do not share a consistent operational picture. IronArc is developing software to bring mission control, perception and shared state together.

01 // COMMAND

Keep operators in control

Bring mission context, platform status and operator decisions into a coherent command workflow.

02 // INTELLIGENCE

Make sensing useful

Connect sensor observations and edge AI perception to the mission, with evidence operators can review.

03 // INTEROPERABILITY

Work across platforms

Connect compatible third-party systems through validated integrations, without tying the architecture to one vehicle manufacturer.

THREE LAYERS.
SHARED PURPOSE.

FORGE commands. TEMPER perceives. KASSCORE is the connective layer for interoperability and shared operational state. FORGE and TEMPER validation comes first; broader integration follows the evidence.
