THE
THESIS.

Energy infrastructure, autonomous systems and critical operations share the same underlying problem: fragmented data, weak evidence continuity and poor visibility into asset state. IronArc is building the layer that connects them.

01 // PROBLEM

Data is not intelligence

Video, telemetry, inspection records and historical context are frequently split across disconnected tools and manual workflows.

02 // PROBLEM

Evidence loses context

A finding without provenance, quality, location, mission state or prior history is difficult to trust and expensive to review.

03 // PROBLEM

Hardware dictates workflow

Operators should not need to replace proven platforms just to gain better intelligence. IronArc is designed to sit above compatible hardware.
