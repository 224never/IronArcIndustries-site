Mission systems under human control

IronArc is building a family of human-operated mission systems around one architecture. Our goal is to bring sensor observations, mission records and operator decisions into one workflow, starting with civil fieldwork.

One architecture, three layers

TEMPER produces advisory observations. FABRIC maintains the canonical mission record and preserves provenance. In FORGE, operators review information and approve every vehicle command and mission action.

TEMPER

Sensing & perception

The sensing node turns sensor inputs into observations for human review. AI remains advisory.

FABRIC

Integration & mission state

The integration layer preserves the authoritative mission record, where observations came from and how the record can be replayed.

FORGE

Operator workspace

The operator workspace supports mission review, mapping, evidence and reporting.

IronArc carriers are human-controlled and human-operated. INAV supports pilot-controlled navigation and safety assistance.

Why human control matters

Field conditions change, sensor readings can be ambiguous, and decisions can affect responders and the public. A human operator can weigh the context, judge whether there is enough evidence to act and take responsibility for the decision.

Authority to act

A designated operator initiates and approves every vehicle command and mission action. Operators must be able to decline a recommendation, intervene or call off a mission.

Information to judge

TEMPER provides advisory observations. We design FORGE to show where observations came from and to make uncertainty and missing or stale information visible before a decision.

Accountability after the decision

We design FORGE and FABRIC to record what the operator reviewed, who authorised the action and what was recorded afterwards, so decisions can be reviewed and lessons applied.

Three offerings built on the same architecture

We are developing three ways to use the IronArc architecture, from the operator’s workspace to a complete sensing and carrier system.

IronArc software

Give operators one place to review mission context, maps, observations and evidence, with a shared record for reporting and replay.

TEMPER sensing nodes

Package sensing and advisory perception in IronArc hardware that provides observations for operator review.

Integrated IronArc carrier systems

Bring an IronArc carrier, TEMPER sensing and mission software together for human-operated fieldwork.

Carrier family in development

The first integrated evaluation is planned around a human-piloted aerial carrier. Ground and surface-vessel carriers are longer-term concepts for the same architecture.

Designed for civil fieldwork

CIVIL is IronArc’s near-term development focus. We are preparing these workflows for supervised evaluation with relevant operators and authorities.

Wildfire situational awareness

Help responders review possible hotspots, incident context and access information for mapping and reporting.

Search and rescue support

Provide operator-reviewed observations and timestamped evidence to support the response led by the competent authorities.

Infrastructure inspection

Bring observations, site context and evidence into a review workflow for infrastructure and industrial teams.

Algeria · CIVIL R&D exploration

In Algeria, we are exploring wildfire awareness and search and rescue as R&D use cases. Any supervised evaluation would depend on readiness, an agreed scope and authorisation from the competent authorities.
